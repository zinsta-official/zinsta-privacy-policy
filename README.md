🔐 Zinsta Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Zinsta (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a modern social networking platform built to help people connect, share moments, and communicate safely. Your privacy matters to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you use Zinsta.

By accessing or using Zinsta, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📌 Information We Collect
👤 Account Information

When you create and use a Zinsta account, we may collect:

A unique user identifier (for authentication)

Username, profile photo, and bio

Account privacy settings (public or private)

Followers and following relationships

This information is essential to operate Zinsta’s core social features.

🖼️ Content You Share

Zinsta stores content that you choose to create or upload, such as:

Posts (images and captions)

Shorts (short-form videos)

Stories

Comments, likes, and interactions

🔒 Private accounts limit content visibility to approved followers only.

💬 Messages & Calls

Zinsta enables private communication between users, including:

One-to-one chat messages

Audio and video call signaling

Message delivery and seen indicators

📞 Call-related data is used only to enable real-time communication and is not recorded or stored permanently.

💘 Dating Features

If you choose to use Zinsta Dating, we may process:

Dating profile information you provide

Dating requests (sent and received)

Match status between users

Dating data is strictly limited to involved users and protected by access controls.

🛡️ Safety & Moderation Data

To maintain a safe and respectful platform, we collect:

Reports submitted by users (posts, comments, or accounts)

Feedback messages

Blocked user relationships

Reports are preserved for moderation and safety review and are visible only to the reporter and authorized administrators.

👁️ How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Deliver and maintain Zinsta’s features

Apply privacy, blocking, and visibility settings

Enable posts, stories, messaging, calls, and dating

Detect, prevent, and address harmful activity

Improve performance, stability, and user experience

🚫 We do not sell your personal data.

🔐 Data Visibility & Access Control

Zinsta is built with privacy-first access rules:

Only signed-in users can access platform content

Private accounts are visible to followers only

Blocked users cannot view profiles, content, or messages

Users control their own content and interactions

Administrative access is limited to moderation purposes

All access is enforced through secure, rule-based systems.

🗂️ Data Storage & Security

Zinsta uses trusted cloud infrastructure provided by Google

Authentication and data storage are securely managed

Strong access rules prevent unauthorized access

No public or open database access is allowed

We follow industry-standard security practices to protect user data.

🗑️ Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can:

Delete posts, shorts, stories, and comments

Block or unblock users at any time

Delete your account, which removes associated data

Certain moderation records may be retained where required for safety or legal compliance.

👶 Children’s Privacy

Zinsta is not intended for children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

🌍 Third-Party Services

Zinsta uses trusted third-party services (such as Google Firebase) to support:

Secure authentication

Real-time data storage

App functionality and notifications

These services operate under their own privacy and security standards.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
