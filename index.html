🔐 Zinsta Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Zinsta (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a modern social networking platform built to help people connect, share moments, and communicate safely. Your privacy matters to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you use Zinsta.

📌 Information We Collect

👤 Account Information

  • Unique user identifier (for authentication)
  • Username, profile photo, and bio
  • Account privacy settings (public or private)
  • Followers and following relationships

🖼️ Content You Share

  • Posts (images and captions)
  • Shorts (short-form videos)
  • Stories
  • Comments, likes, and interactions

Private accounts restrict content visibility to approved followers only.

💬 Messages & Calls

  • One-to-one chat messages
  • Audio and video call signaling
  • Message delivery and seen indicators

Call-related data is used only to enable real-time communication and is not recorded or stored permanently.

💘 Dating Features

  • Dating profile information
  • Dating requests (sent and received)
  • Match status between users

Dating data is visible only to involved users.

🛡️ Safety & Moderation

  • User reports (posts, comments, accounts)
  • Feedback messages
  • Blocked user relationships

Reports are retained for safety and moderation purposes.

🔐 Data Storage & Security

  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Protected authentication systems
  • Strict access control rules
  • No public database access

🗑️ Data Control

  • Delete posts, stories, shorts, and comments
  • Block or unblock users
  • Delete your account at any time

👶 Children’s Privacy

Zinsta is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

🌍 Third-Party Services

Zinsta uses trusted third-party services such as Google Firebase for authentication, data storage, and notifications.
